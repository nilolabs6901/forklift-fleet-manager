## Fleet Shield ICP — ALWAYS REFERENCE THIS

Fleet Shield is a B2B SaaS product for forklift fleet management. ICP = organizations operating forklift/material-handling fleets large enough that manual management has become a cost and risk center.

**Firmographics:** Warehousing, distribution, third-party logistics (3PL), and manufacturing operations. Sized by truck count (fleet size), not revenue or headcount. Single large facilities or multi-site operators. US-based to start.

**Buyer/user personas:** Operations leaders, fleet/facility managers, warehouse and distribution center managers, supply chain and logistics directors, and C-suite/ownership at smaller operators. Day-to-day users handle maintenance scheduling, vendor invoice approval, and uptime. Practical, operations-minded buyers, not technical buyers. Keep messaging concrete and outcome-focused.

**Problems solved:** Forklift downtime and unplanned breakdowns; reactive instead of predictive maintenance; hard-to-verify vendor invoices with overcharges and discrepancies; no single source of truth for fleet costs, maintenance history, and risk; manual spreadsheet tracking that does not scale.

**Capabilities:** AI invoice parsing and discrepancy detection; predictive maintenance; fleet risk scoring engine; WhatsApp/SMS field agent; centralized fleet, cost, and maintenance visibility.

**Poor fit / disqualifiers:** Operations with only a handful of trucks; businesses with no in-house material-handling fleet; price-only buyers indifferent to downtime or invoice accuracy.

**Positioning edge:** Built by people from inside the forklift dealership world, so it targets the real cost leaks (downtime plus invoice overcharges) rather than generic fleet software.
